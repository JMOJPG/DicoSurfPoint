Before You Travel

Here’s everything you need to know to plan your stay at Dico Surf Point — from surf conditions to travel logistics and what to bring.

✈️ Getting Here

Fly into Medan (Kualanamu Airport), then take a domestic flight to Sinabang, Simeulue.

We can help arrange transfers from the airport directly to the lodge.

🌊 Surf Conditions

Consistent reef breaks suitable for intermediate to advanced surfers.

Best season: April to October.

🏄 Board Rentals

Limited surfboards available at the lodge.

We strongly recommend bringing your own board for best performance.

🍽️ Food & Meals

Daily meals included with fresh Indonesian food and simple Western options.

Dietary requirements can be accommodated if requested in advance.

📶 Internet

Limited connectivity — expect a digital detox.

Telkomsel SIM cards work best on the island.

💰 Money

Bring cash from Medan. ATMs are unreliable on the island.

Card payments are not accepted in most places.

🛟 Safety

Reef breaks can be shallow — reef boots recommended.

Always surf within your ability level.

🎒 What to Bring

  • Surfboard
  • Reef boots
  • Sunscreen
  • Cash
  • Power bank
  • Light clothing
  • Travel insurance

🕒 Best Time

April to October is peak surf season with consistent swell and offshore winds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own board?

Recommended, but rentals are available in limited supply.

Is food included?

Yes — daily meals are included with your stay.

Is it beginner friendly?

Mainly intermediate to advanced waves, but some beginner-friendly conditions exist.

How do I get there?

Fly to Medan, then connect to Sinabang Airport. We arrange pickup.

About the Lodge

Dico Surf Point was created to offer a simpler surf experience — uncrowded waves, natural living, and connection to the ocean.
