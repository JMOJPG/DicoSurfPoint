Travel Information

Everything you need to know before your trip to Simeulue.

✈️ Getting Here

Fly into Medan (Kualanamu International Airport), then take a domestic flight to Sinabang, Simeulue.

We can assist with airport transfers directly to the lodge. Travel schedules can change due to weather, so flexibility is recommended.

🌊 Surf Conditions

Simeulue offers consistent, uncrowded reef breaks suitable for intermediate to advanced surfers.

Best swell season runs from April to October, but waves can be found year-round.

🏄 Board Rentals

We offer a limited selection of surfboards for guests who do not travel with their own gear.

For best performance, we recommend bringing your own boards suited to reef conditions.

🍽️ Food & Meals

Fresh daily meals are included at the lodge, featuring local Indonesian dishes and simple international options.

We can cater to basic dietary needs if informed in advance.

📶 Internet & Signal

Limited connectivity on the island. Messaging apps generally work, but expect a digital detox experience.

Telkomsel SIM cards provide the most reliable coverage.

💰 Money & Payments

Bring enough cash from Medan. ATMs on the island are limited and unreliable.

Card payments are not widely accepted.

🛟 Safety & Surf Etiquette

Reef breaks can be shallow — reef boots are recommended.

Always surf within your ability and respect local surf conditions.

🎒 What to Bring

  • Surfboards (recommended)
  • Reef boots
  • High SPF sunscreen
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Cash
  • Power bank
  • Light clothing
  • Travel insurance

🕒 Best Time to Visit

April to October offers the most consistent swell and offshore winds.

The off-season is quieter with fewer surfers and a more relaxed atmosphere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own surfboard?

We recommend bringing your own board for best performance, but limited rentals are available at the lodge.

Is the lodge suitable for beginners?

Simeulue is best suited for intermediate to advanced surfers, but some waves may suit confident beginners.

Is food included?

Yes, daily meals are included with your stay, prepared fresh each day.

How do I get from the airport to the lodge?

We arrange pickups from Sinabang Airport directly to the lodge.

Is there internet at the lodge?

Internet is limited, making it a great place to disconnect and focus on surfing and relaxation.

🌊 About the Owner

Originally from Japan, Naoto Mogi has spent over 20 years immersed in surf culture, shaping boards and chasing waves around the world.

Now based near Bells Beach in Australia, he created Dico Surf Point to share a simpler surf experience — uncrowded waves, connection to nature, and time away from modern distractions.
