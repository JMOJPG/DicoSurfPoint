Before You Travel

Here’s everything you need to know to plan your stay at Dico Surf Point — from surf conditions to travel logistics and what to bring.

✈️ Getting Here

Fly to Medan (Kualanamu Airport), then take a domestic flight to Sinabang, Simeulue.

We can arrange airport pickup and take you directly to Dico Surf Point.

🌊 Surf Conditions

Simeulue offers a variety of waves and surf spots within easy reach of the lodge.

Surf is available year-round. March to October generally brings bigger swells, while the other months often offer smaller, cleaner and less crowded conditions.

🏄 Board Rentals

A limited selection of surfboards is available for guests.

You’re welcome to bring your own board, and we can also help arrange surfboard transport from Medan to Simeulue.

🍽️ Food & Meals

We serve fresh local Indonesian food at the lodge.

If you have dietary preferences or requirements — such as no fish, no meat or vegetarian meals — just let us know before your stay and we’ll do our best to adjust the meals for you.

📶 Internet

Internet is available, but connections can sometimes be slower or less reliable than on the mainland.

Telkomsel generally provides the best mobile coverage on Simeulue.

💰 Money

We recommend bringing enough Indonesian rupiah for your stay.

ATMs are available on Simeulue, but cash is useful for small shops, local businesses and other expenses.

🛟 Safety

Surf conditions can change quickly and some reefs can be shallow.

Always surf within your ability and ask our local team for advice about suitable spots and conditions.

🎒 What to Bring

  • Surfboard if you have one
  • Reef boots
  • Sunscreen
  • Indonesian rupiah
  • Power bank
  • Light clothing
  • Personal medication
  • Travel insurance

☀️ Best Time to Visit

Simeulue can be enjoyed year-round.

March to October usually brings larger swells, but conditions can also be bigger, windier and busier. The other months often bring smaller, cleaner waves with fewer surfers — conditions many of our guests prefer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own surfboard?

Bringing your own board is recommended, but we have a limited selection of rental boards available for guests.

Are meals available?

Yes. We serve local Indonesian food at the lodge. If you don’t eat fish or meat, are vegetarian, or have other dietary requirements, please let us know before arrival and we’ll do our best to adjust the meals for you.

Is Dico Surf Point suitable for beginners?

Yes. We can help beginners find suitable conditions and surf spots depending on the swell and weather. More experienced surfers also have access to many quality waves around Simeulue.

How do I get there?

Fly to Medan (Kualanamu Airport), then take a domestic flight to Sinabang, Simeulue. We can arrange airport pickup.

About the Lodge

Dico Surf Point is a relaxed, family-run surf lodge in Simeulue. We focus on friendly hospitality, comfortable stays and helping guests enjoy the island at their own pace. Whether you’re surfing, exploring or simply relaxing by the ocean, our local team is here to help make your stay easy and memorable.
