Travel Information

Everything you need to know before visiting Simeulue.

✈️ How to Get Here

Fly into Medan (Kualanamu International Airport), then take a domestic flight to Sinabang Airport (Simeulue). Flights operate on limited schedules and may be weather dependent. Aircraft are small, so luggage limits apply.

Our team can assist with airport pickup and transport to the lodge.

🍺 Alcohol & Local Culture

Simeulue is a conservative Muslim island. Alcohol availability is limited and prices are higher due to import restrictions.

We kindly ask guests to respect local customs by dressing modestly in town and behaving respectfully in public areas.

💰 Money & Payments

ATMs are limited and sometimes unreliable. We strongly recommend bringing sufficient cash from Medan.

Most shops and services do not accept card payments or digital wallets.

📶 Internet & Signal

Mobile signal can be limited in certain areas of the island. Messaging usually works, but high-speed internet is not guaranteed.

Telkomsel SIM cards offer the best coverage in the region.

🎒 What to Bring

  • Reef boots
  • High SPF sunscreen
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Travel insurance
  • Cash
  • Power bank
  • Light rain jacket
  • Personal medications
