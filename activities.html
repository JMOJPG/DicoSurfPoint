Island Life

Surf. Slow down. Explore Simeulue.

More Than Just Surf

Life at Dico Surf Point moves with the ocean. Between surf sessions, the island offers quiet roads, empty beaches, reef breaks, fishing trips, and time to disconnect fully.

What You Can Do

Surfing

Uncrowded reef breaks with consistent waves year-round.

Island Exploration

Scooter rides through quiet villages and coastal roads.

Snorkelling

Clear waters and untouched reefs just offshore.

Sunsets

Golden hour sessions on empty beaches.

Daily Rhythm

Wake up with the ocean, surf when the tide is right, relax in between sessions, and finish the day watching the sun drop into the horizon. No rush. No crowds.
