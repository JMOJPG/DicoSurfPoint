Things to Do

Guided surf tours, reef exploring, a scenic local waterfall, fishing trips, cafes, and much more await you in the untouched paradise of Simeulue. Whether you're paddling out for your first wave, snorkeling vibrant coral reefs, riding around on a scooter, or chasing an epic sunset from the shore, there's something here for all walks of life from solo travellers to families. Our experiences are guided by locals who know the island inside and out, offering you a deeper connection to both nature and culture. If there are any things that may of piqued your interests and anything else you can think of ask our manager Robi.
