Life on Simeulue

From uncrowded reef breaks to slow scooter rides through the village, Simeulue offers more than just world-class waves. Guests can enjoy guided surf sessions, fishing trips, snorkeling crystal-clear reefs, coastal exploring, sunset missions, and cultural experiences guided by locals who know the island inside out.

Whether you’re here to chase swell or simply disconnect and reset, Dico Surf Point offers a laid-back base to experience the island at your own pace.
