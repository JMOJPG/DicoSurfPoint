Life on Simeulue

From uncrowded reef breaks to scooter rides through quiet villages, Simeulue offers more than just world-class waves.

Guests can enjoy guided surf sessions, fishing trips, snorkeling pristine reefs, coastal exploration, sunset adventures, and authentic cultural experiences.

Whether chasing swell or taking time to reset, Dico Surf Point is your base for island life.
