Life on Simeulue

From uncrowded reef breaks to slow scooter rides through the village, Simeulue offers more than just world-class waves.

Guests can enjoy guided surf sessions, fishing trips, snorkeling crystal-clear reefs, coastal exploring, sunset missions, and authentic local cultural experiences.

Whether you're chasing swell or resetting from the world, Dico Surf Point is your base.
