About Dico Surf Point

Tucked away on the remote island of Simeulue, Dico Surf Point is built for surfers chasing something real. Empty lineups, warm water, and a place that feels like home after every session.

Named after a loyal companion, the lodge carries that same spirit — freedom, adventure, and connection. Whether you're here to score waves or switch off completely, this is your base.
