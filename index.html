Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Simeulue?

Fly to Medan, then take a domestic flight or ferry to Simeulue. We can help arrange transfers.

Do you provide food?

Yes, we offer fresh daily meals with local Indonesian cuisine.

Is it good for beginners?

Simeulue has waves for different levels, but it is best suited for intermediate to advanced surfers.

When is the best surf season?

March to October is typically the most consistent surf season.
