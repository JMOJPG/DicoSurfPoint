Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get there?

Fly to Medan, then take a domestic flight to Simeulue. Airport pick-up and drop-off in Simeulue are included with your stay.

What is included in my stay?

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included, along with drinking water, coffee and tea, use of a scooter, and one massage per guest during your stay. Dirt bikes are also available for an additional fee.

Can I rent a surfboard?

Yes. Surfboard rental is available at Dico Surf Point. Please contact us for available boards and prices.

Can you help transport my surfboard from Medan?

Yes. We can help arrange surfboard transport by ship from Medan to Simeulue. This can be cheaper than taking your board on the flight. Prices can change, so please contact us for the current cost.

When is the best time to surf in Simeulue?

Simeulue offers surf year-round. March to October generally brings bigger and more consistent swell, while the quieter months often offer smaller, cleaner waves, more surf spot options and fewer crowds.
